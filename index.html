About My Work & Craft

Welcome to Aarya Soul Band. As an independent composer, lyricist, and producer, I dedicate my passion to crafting original Kannada melodies that touch emotions and resonate deeply with listeners.

In today's Kannada independent music scene, creating high-quality original content requires distinct songwriting, meaningful lyric writing, and meticulous musical arrangements. My mission is to give genuine hit tracks to the independent Kannada artist space.

  • Original Music Composition: Writing acoustic, emotional, and devotional melodies built from the ground up.
  • Lyric Writing: Penning authentic Kannada lyrics that express real life, love, relationships, and devotion.
  • Independent Hits: Delivering popular hits like Helikodu O Devare (113K+ views) and Nan Devathe (81K+ views).
