About Aarya Soul Band

Aarya Soul Band is an independent Kannada music identity focused on original songs, meaningful lyrics, emotional storytelling and musical experimentation.

The catalogue moves across devotional and spiritual themes, love and heartbreak, life experiences and contemporary Kannada sounds. The aim is to create songs that communicate an emotion clearly and connect with listeners beyond a single genre.

Santhosh S R is the Music Director associated with Aarya Soul Band. The project brings together songwriting, music direction, vocal performance, production ideas and visual storytelling around independent Kannada music releases.

The official YouTube channel @AaryaSoulBand is the primary video destination for the band's songs, lyrical videos, music releases and future creative work.
